### Chosen stack (to be confirmed in RESEARCH.md)
| Need | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Build | Vite 8, vanilla **TypeScript**, static `dist/` | Brief requires it; fast; code splitting |
| 3D | three r186 (tree-shaken ESM imports) | Standard; InstancedMesh for 100k+ particles |
| Post-FX | **pmndrs postprocessing** | Merges effects into few passes (important on mobile); built-in Bloom and ChromaticAberration, plus **custom `Effect` subclasses** for CRT scanlines, grain, glitch and datamosh |

| Fonts | @fontsource (OFL), self-hosted: **Space Grotesk** (display), **JetBrains Mono** (HUD/terminal), **Noto Sans JP** 700 subset (kanji) | Open licenses; bundled by Vite and excluded from the JS size budget |

### World layout and camera
- **Single path.** One continuous CatmullRom path through a single world, laid out along −Z:
  1. Sky, y≈220, descending through cloud planes to the rooftop at y≈60 (0–20%)
  2. Drop off the roof edge into the alley at street level (20–40%)
  3. Particles dissolve the alley; the camera enters the tunnel (40–60%)
  4. Tunnel exit, orbit around the floating shrine (60–80%)
  5. Rise to y≈300 facing the eastern sunrise (80–100%)
- **Performance.** Scene groups show only near their chapter range, so there are no hidden draw calls.
- **Transitions.** Glitch bursts fire when a chapter boundary is crossed; the datamosh runs at the 2→3 boundary.
- **Reduced motion.** `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off Lenis smoothing. The camera holds on a still pose per chapter, and changing chapters triggers a 600 ms crossfade on a CSS black overlay instead of a flight. Glitch, datamosh, shockwave shake and title scramble are disabled; the title just fades. Rain slows.
- **Fallback.** If WebGL fails or context creation throws, `html.no-webgl` shows CSS gradient chapter backdrops. All text, nav and the terminal keep working.

### Adaptive quality tiers
| Tier | Pixel ratio | Particles | Post-FX |
|---|---|---|---|
| high | ≤ 2 | 120k | full |
| mid | 1.5 | 40k | bloom at half resolution, no datamosh feedback |
| low / mobile | 1 | 15k | bloom plus a light CRT/grain pass only |

A runtime governor steps the tier down after 2 s of low FPS.

## Verification
1. `npm run build` must be green (`tsc --noEmit && vite build`).
2. **`scripts/verify.mjs`:**
   - Starts `vite preview` on port 4173 and launches Chromium with `--use-angle=swiftshader --enable-unsafe-swiftshader` so WebGL works headless.
   - For each viewport (1440×900, 390×844): loads the page, scrolls to each chapter's midpoint, waits for frames to settle, and saves `verify/screenshots/{desktop|mobile}-0N-<chapter>.png`. That makes 10 screenshots.
   - Counts `console` errors and `pageerror` events.
   - Repeats both viewports with `reducedMotion: 'reduce'`, saving screenshots to `verify/screenshots/reduced/`, and counts errors again.
   - Presses backtick, types `help`, and asserts the output lists `jack-in`, `glitch` and `rain off`.
   - Also checks that the canvas isn't blank by sampling pixel variance for each screenshot.
   - Prints a summary table and exits non-zero on any error.
